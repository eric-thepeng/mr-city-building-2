How to play. Grab a building from your hand (the row in front of you) and place it on the terrain. Support beams must reach the ground. Each beam's length costs money. Buildings near the right neighbours earn money. Look at the green radius and the +/− badges.
Hands (Specs, Quest): pinch near a building to carry it, or pinch while pointing at it to pull it along the ray. Let go to build.
Controllers (Quest): trigger or grip to grab, thumbstick to push/pull.
Mouse: drag buildings, wheel = height, Q/E = rotate, drag empty space = look around.
